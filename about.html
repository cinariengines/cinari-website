ABOUT CINARI

An intelligence layer for infrastructure.

Cinari is an open-source network intelligence engine built around a simple idea: infrastructure failures are rarely isolated events. The useful answer lives in the relationships between them.

Our goal

Make complex infrastructure behavior understandable to the engineers responsible for it. Cinari is designed to move beyond disconnected metrics, logs, traces, and alerts toward a coherent explanation of what happened and why it likely happened.

What Cinari is not

Cinari is not intended to be a replacement for every monitoring, logging, tracing, or incident-management system. It is an intelligence engine that can work alongside existing infrastructure and data sources.

Principles

Evidence over guesses

Every diagnosis should be tied to observable signals.

Uncertainty is information

When evidence is incomplete, Cinari should say so.

Minimal integration

Useful infrastructure tooling should not require rebuilding the system around it.

Open by default

The core engine is built in the open for inspection, contribution, and trust.

LONG-TERM VISION

From signals to system understanding.

As Cinari evolves, the engine is intended to build richer models of infrastructure behavior—helping engineers investigate incidents faster and understand the causal relationships hidden across distributed systems.
