THE PROBLEM

Monitoring tells you something broke.
Cinari is built to investigate why.

01

Observe

Capture meaningful network and system behavior with minimal disruption to existing infrastructure.

02

Correlate

Connect events across services, processes, hosts, and network paths instead of viewing signals in isolation.

03

Explain

Construct a likely failure chain and show the evidence behind the diagnosis, including uncertainty and alternatives.

DESIGNED FOR ENGINEERS

Integrate first.
Understand later.

Run Cinari alongside the infrastructure you already have. The goal is not to replace every observability system—it is to add an intelligence layer that can reason across the signals your system produces.

C
#include <cinari/cinari.h>

cinari_init();
cinari_observe(system);
cinari_analyze(events);
cinari_shutdown();
